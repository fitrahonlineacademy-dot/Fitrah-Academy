What children can learn

Learning for mind, heart, and future.

Subject content is adapted to each child's age, current ability, and learning needs.

01

Islamic & Arabic Studies

Build familiarity with Arabic and age-appropriate Islamic knowledge.

  • Arabic alphabet and letter recognition
  • Pronunciation and reading foundations
  • Basic Arabic language
  • Qur'an reading foundations
  • Du'as, Islamic basics, and good manners
02

English Language

Develop communication and literacy skills step by step.

  • Phonics and sound recognition
  • Vocabulary and reading comprehension
  • Writing, spelling, and sentence construction
  • Speaking and listening
  • Everyday communication skills
03

Mathematics Foundation

Build number confidence, reasoning, and practical problem-solving.

  • Number recognition and number sense
  • Counting and number relationships
  • Basic addition and subtraction
  • Multiplication and division when appropriate
  • Shapes, patterns, and problem-solving

The specific content offered will be discussed and confirmed with the parent or guardian before lessons begin.
